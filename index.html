Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle plumbing emergencies?
Yes. Burst pipes, major leaks, and sewer backups don't keep business hours, and neither do we when it counts. Call (540) 497-3174 and we'll walk you through shutting off the water and get to you as fast as we can.
What areas do you serve?
We're based in Front Royal and serve Warren County and nearby Northern Shenandoah Valley communities. If you're close by, call us — odds are good we can help.
Do you charge for estimates?
No — project estimates are free. We'll look at the job, explain your options in plain English, and give you a fair price before any work begins.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes. Dogwood Plumbing LLC is a licensed and insured Virginia plumbing contractor. We're happy to provide documentation on request.
What forms of payment do you accept?
We accept major credit cards along with the usual options. If you have questions about payment, just ask when you schedule.
Do you offer any discounts?
Yes — 10% off for active-duty military, veterans, and first responders. It's our way of saying thank you.
