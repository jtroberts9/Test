Plumbing With Integrity, From Our Family to Yours

Dogwood Plumbing LLC is a family-operated, veteran-owned business dedicated to serving our community with the highest level of integrity and expertise. With over six years of experience, our team specializes in all phases of residential plumbing — from service and installation to repair.

We take immense pride in the attention to detail we bring to every project. That commitment to quality workmanship has earned us a reputation for excellence with more than a thousand customers around Front Royal — and neighbors who recommend us to their friends.

The military taught us to show up on time, tell it straight, and never leave a job half done. Those habits came home with us, and they're in every water heater we set, every drain we clear, and every pipe we sweat.

Why "Dogwood"?

The flowering dogwood is Virginia's state flower and state tree — hardy, dependable, and blooming right here in the Shenandoah Valley every spring. That felt like the right name for a company built on local roots and work that holds up season after season.

When you see the dogwood blossom on our estimate, you know what's behind it: a local family that answers its own phone, stands behind its own work, and treats your home like our own.
